Project Overview

The Letter App is a digital platform that brings back the art of letter writing in the modern age. It allows users to create, share, and discover meaningful letters that create lasting connections and preserve memories for future generations.

The design focuses on creating an intimate and personal experience that captures the essence of traditional letter writing while leveraging modern technology to enhance sharing and discovery. The app encourages thoughtful communication and meaningful connections in our fast-paced digital world.
